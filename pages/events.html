IEEE TechFest 2026

Organised by the IEEE Student Branch, RRCE, in association with the IEEE Bangalore Section. A day to promote technological innovation, knowledge sharing and engineering excellence among students: keynotes in the morning, competitions through the afternoon, results at the close.

  • Prof. Devendra Gowda Patil, Chair, Student Activities Committee, IEEE Bangalore Section
  • Dr. A. P. Jyothi, Post-Doctoral Research Fellow, Thailand; IEEE Senior Member
  • Ms. Savitha Pareek, Senior Solutions Assistant, NVIS; AI infrastructure and GPU benchmarking
  • Dr. Manjunath T. C., Ph.D. (IIT Bombay); IEEE Senior Member and Student Branch Counsellor, RRCE

Web Designing

Design and build a responsive website on a theme revealed at the venue. Individual or teams of up to two. Coordinators: Mrudula S and Mukesh KU.

  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript only; AI tools permitted
  • Must work on desktop, tablet and mobile; every link and form functional
