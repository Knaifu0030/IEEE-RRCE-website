A global body. A local branch.

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organisation. RRCE is an autonomous engineering college on Mysore Road. The IEEE Student Branch at RRCE is where the two meet: a student-run chapter with a faculty counsellor, reporting to the IEEE Bangalore Section.

What each one brings.

The world's largest technical professional organisation

Formed in 1963 when the American Institute of Electrical Engineers (1884) merged with the Institute of Radio Engineers (1912). Today IEEE publishes a large share of the world's literature in electrical engineering, computing and electronics, and develops the standards behind everyday technology, including the 802.11 family that Wi-Fi runs on.

  • 400,000+ members across 160+ countries
  • 39 technical societies, from Computer to Power & Energy
  • IEEE Xplore: millions of papers, standards and proceedings
  • Region 10 (Asia Pacific), IEEE Bangalore Section established 1977

An autonomous engineering college in Bengaluru

RajaRajeswari College of Engineering was established in 2006 by the Moogambigai Charitable and Education Trust. Approved by AICTE and affiliated to VTU, the college is autonomous, accredited by NAAC and NBA, and ISO 9001:2015 certified. Its campus sits at Ramohalli Cross, Kumbalgodu, on Mysore Road.

  • 2,297+ students and 1,800+ placements
  • 22+ student clubs, from Coding to Robotics
  • AICTE IDEA Lab and an Institution's Innovation Council
  • Motto: Learn, Explore, Invent

IEEE RRCE, under the IEEE Bangalore Section.

The IEEE Student Branch at RajaRajeswari College of Engineering belongs to the IEEE Bangalore Section, one of the most active sections in Region 10 (Asia Pacific). Student members run the branch; a faculty counsellor represents it to the college and the Section.

Two histories, one line.

IEEE's story starts in 1884. RRCE's starts in 2006. In 2026 the two share a stage at IEEE TechFest.

1884

AIEE founded

The American Institute of Electrical Engineers forms in New York, in the era of the telegraph and electric light.

1912

IRE founded

The Institute of Radio Engineers forms to serve the new field of wireless.

1963

IEEE formed

AIEE and IRE merge into the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers.

1977

IEEE Bangalore Section established

The Section that IEEE RRCE reports to today, part of Region 10 (Asia Pacific).

2006

RRCE established

RajaRajeswari College of Engineering opens at Kumbalgodu, Mysore Road, under the Moogambigai Charitable and Education Trust.

2026

IEEE TechFest 2026 at RRCE

The branch hosts TechFest with the IEEE Bangalore Section as part of the IEEE Day celebrations: keynotes, talks and competitions on 31 October.
