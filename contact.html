Kumbalgodu, Mysore Road

Visit us

On campus, off Mysore Road.

The college sits at Ramohalli Cross in Kumbalgodu, on the Bengaluru to Mysuru highway, about 20 km from the city centre. Buses on the Mysore Road corridor stop at Kumbalgodu; the campus is a short walk from the cross.

For admissions and general enquiries the college office answers on the landline, +91 80 2843 7375, and on the mobile numbers 90088 45678 and 99000 58214.
