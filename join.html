Membership

Become a member.

Two steps, one afternoon. Join IEEE for the global membership, then apply to the branch so we know you are on campus. Open to every department at RRCE.

Two paths

Global membership. Local branch.

IEEE membership is held with IEEE directly. Branch membership is how we count you, contact you and sign you up for events here.

Path one

Join IEEE (global)

  1. Create an IEEE account at ieee.org/join.
  2. Choose Student membership and select RajaRajeswari College of Engineering as your institution.
  3. Add society memberships such as the Computer Society or Women in Engineering if you want them.

Dues are set by IEEE and vary by region and year. The current amount is shown at checkout on ieee.org.

Join on ieee.org →

Path two

Join IEEE RRCE (the branch)

Fill in the application below with your USN, department and interests. The executive committee reviews it with the Branch Counsellor, adds you to the branch roster and the group chat, and tells you about the next event.

Already an IEEE member? Apply anyway so the branch can link your IEEE number to RRCE.

Apply to the branch →

FAQ

Questions we get every semester.

Who can join?
Any student at RRCE, from any department and any year. Undergraduate and postgraduate students are both eligible for IEEE student membership.
Do I need to be in ECE or CSE?
No. IEEE covers computing, electronics, power, robotics, biomedical engineering, management and more. Civil, MBA, MCA and science students are welcome.
What does it cost?
Membership dues are set by IEEE and depend on your region and the time of year you join. The current amount is shown when you sign up at ieee.org/membership/join. Branch activities on campus are free for members unless an event says otherwise.
How do I get my IEEE number?
Your IEEE member number is issued when you complete the signup on ieee.org. It appears in your IEEE account and on your digital membership card. Share it with the branch through the form so we can link you to RRCE.
Can I join IEEE societies too?
Yes. Society memberships such as the Computer Society, Women in Engineering (WIE) and the Robotics and Automation Society can be added when you join or at any time from your IEEE account.
